About Tropi Greek

Aguadilla flavor with its own identity

Tropi Greek brings together Puerto Rican favorites, Greek-inspired touches and a menu built around wraps, snacks, burgers, salads and drinks that people actually want to order.

It’s casual, colorful, flavorful and built to feel like a real local spot, not a generic concept trying too hard to sound cool.

What makes it different

  • Pita wraps, pitadillas, quesadillas and layered nachos
  • Frappes, margaritas, daiquiris and refreshing drinks
  • Greek-inspired touches mixed with Puerto Rican flavor
  • A menu built around real favorites people actually order
